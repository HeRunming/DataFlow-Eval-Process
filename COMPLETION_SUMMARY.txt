                        ARCHITECTURE OVERVIEW
================================================================================

PIPELINE WORKFLOW
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

  Raw JSONL (MC questions)
         ↓
    [Step 0] Preprocess
         ↓
    [Step 1] Rewrite (LLM)
         ↓
    [Step 2] Leak Check (LLM Judge)
         ↓
    [Step 3] Dedup (N-gram)
         ↓
    [Step 4] Align Fields
         ↓
    [Step 5] Tag Subject (LLM)
         ↓
    Cleaned, reformatted OE dataset

COMPONENT INTERACTIONS
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

  StemMCPreprocessorFilter
    ├── Input: raw JSONL with "question" and "text" fields
    ├── Process: normalize, extract, fix, parse
    └── Output: question_clean, answer_label, options[]

  StemMCToOpenEndedRewriterGenerator
    ├── Input: question_clean, answer_label
    ├── Process: LLM call with bilingual prompts
    └── Output: question_rewritten

  StemMCAnswerLeakFilter
    ├── Input: question_rewritten, answer_label, options[]
    ├── Process: 5-dimensional leak detection
    └── Output: filtered (or kept) records

  [3] ReasoningAnswerNgramFilter, [4] StemColumnAlignGenerator,
  [5] StemSubjectTaggerSampleEvaluator
    └── Standard library operators (already available)
